Questions

What is Selah?
Selah is a private journaling app with an AI friend. You can write reflections, talk things through, and over time it learns how you think and speak so it can be a good friend.
What does “Selah” mean?
It is a Hebrew word found in the Psalms and in Habakkuk, usually read as a call to pause and consider what was just said. To intentionally take time to reflect.
Who can read my journal?
Only you. Your reflections and chats are stored under your signed-in account. The full privacy policy explains how your information is handled.
What does Selah remember, and can I change it?
Selah keeps short memories about you and private notes on your personality and writing style. All of it is visible in Settings, where you can correct or delete it. Deleted memories are not learned again.
Will it bring up things I would rather not revisit?
Not on its own. Prompts, “pick up where you left off” and Feed cards are filtered so heavy topics like grief, illness or crisis are not resurfaced unprompted. In a chat, you decide what to talk about.
Is Selah a replacement for therapy?
No. Selah is a tool for reflection, not a therapist, a medical service or a crisis line. If you are in the United States and in crisis, call or text 988. If you are in immediate danger, call 911 or your local emergency number.
Can I delete my journal or account?
Yes. In Settings you can see and delete anything Selah remembers about you, export everything you have written, or delete your account, which erases your entries, chats and all profile-related data.
How much does Selah cost?
Selah is free to start, with a daily allowance of chat messages. Paid is $7 a month and gives you about five times the daily chat allowance, plus voice chat. You can cancel any time. The pricing page has the full comparison.
Where can I use Selah?
Selah works in your web browser. Soon it will be available as a mobile app for iPhone and Android, with your reflections and chats in sync across all devices.
Why are there no streaks?
By design. Selah is meant to be a place to pause, not another thing to keep up with.
